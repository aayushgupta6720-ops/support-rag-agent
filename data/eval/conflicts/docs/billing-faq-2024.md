# Billing FAQ (2024)

Last updated: 12 March 2024.

**Can I get a refund on an annual plan?** Yes. Annual plans can be refunded
within 30 days of purchase. Contact support with your order ID.

**What happens if a payment fails?** We retry the payment twice over three
days. If both retries fail, the account is downgraded to the free tier.

**When am I charged?** Subscriptions renew automatically on the same day each
billing cycle.
