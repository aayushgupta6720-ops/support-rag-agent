# Invoices and Receipts

An invoice is created every time your subscription renews and is emailed to
your billing contact. You can download any past invoice as a PDF from
Account > Billing > Invoices.

The billing contact doesn't have to be the email you sign in with. Change it
under Account > Billing; future invoices and payment notices go to the new
address.

To show a company name, address, or VAT/tax ID on your invoices, add them on
the same Billing page. The details apply to invoices issued from then on. To
have an earlier invoice reissued with them, open a support ticket with the
invoice number.

Charges appear on your card statement as "EXAMPLE.COM SUBSCRIPTION". When a
refund is issued, a credit note for the refunded amount is added to your
invoice list.
