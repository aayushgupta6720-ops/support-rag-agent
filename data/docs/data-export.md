# Exporting Your Data

You can download a copy of your data from Account > Settings > Export Data.
Choose what to include: tickets, uploaded files, and your account activity
log. The export is packaged as a ZIP file.

We email you a download link when the export is ready. Most exports are ready
within an hour; large accounts can take up to 24 hours. The link stays valid
for 7 days, after which you'll need to request a new export.

Only one export can be in progress at a time for each account.

If you're planning to delete your account, export your data first: once the
deletion grace period ends, deleted data can't be recovered.

To export data programmatically, use the API's bulk export endpoints, which
have their own lower rate limit (see API Rate Limits).
