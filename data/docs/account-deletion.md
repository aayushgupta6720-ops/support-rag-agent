# Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account from Account > Settings > Delete Account. This
requires re-entering your password and confirming a warning about data loss.

Deletion is not immediate: your account and data enter a 30-day grace period
during which you can log back in to cancel the deletion. After 30 days, all
account data, including tickets, uploaded files, and API keys, is
permanently erased and cannot be recovered.

If your account has an active paid subscription, deleting the account
cancels future billing but does not refund the current billing period unless
it falls within the refund window described in the billing policy.

Team/organization accounts can only be deleted by the account owner; other
members must be removed or transferred first.
