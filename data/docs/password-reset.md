# Resetting Your Password

If you're locked out of your account, go to the login page and click "Forgot
password?". Enter the email address on your account and we'll send a reset
link that's valid for 30 minutes.

If the email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder.
Password reset emails are sent from no-reply@example.com, so make sure that
address isn't blocked or filtered.

Once you set a new password, you'll be signed out of all other active
sessions on your account for security. You will need to log back in on any
other devices.

If you no longer have access to the email on file, contact support with a
government-issued ID so we can verify your identity before updating the
account email.
