# Email Notifications

Choose which emails you receive under Account > Notifications. You can turn
off ticket update emails, the weekly usage summary, and product announcements.

Security and billing emails can't be turned off, because they're about access
to your account or money: password reset links, alerts about new sign-ins,
failed payment notices, and receipts.

Notifications are sent from notifications@example.com, and security emails
from no-reply@example.com. If you aren't receiving our emails, check your
spam or junk folder, add both addresses to your contacts or allowlist, and
confirm the email address on your profile under Account > Profile is correct.

Announcement emails include an unsubscribe link at the bottom, which turns
off announcements only.
