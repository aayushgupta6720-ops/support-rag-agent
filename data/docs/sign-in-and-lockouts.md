# Sign-In Problems and Lockouts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts in a row, sign-in to your account is paused
for 15 minutes. The pause ends on its own, and support can't lift it early.
If you've forgotten your password, reset it instead of trying again (see
Resetting Your Password).

We email you when your account is signed in to from a new device or browser.
If you don't recognize a sign-in, reset your password straight away and turn
on two-factor authentication.

Sessions stay signed in until you sign out, or until 30 days pass without any
activity on that device.

If the sign-in page keeps reloading or says your session expired, clear your
browser's cookies for our site and try again. Browser extensions that block
cookies can also stop sign-in from completing.
