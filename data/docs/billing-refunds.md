# Billing and Refunds

Subscriptions renew automatically on the same day each billing cycle, using
the payment method on file. You can view your next billing date under
Account > Billing.

We offer full refunds for annual plans within 14 days of purchase, no
questions asked. Monthly plans are refundable within 7 days. To request a
refund, open a support ticket with your order ID and the reason for the
request; refunds are processed within 5-10 business days back to the
original payment method.

If a charge fails, we retry the payment three times over five days before
downgrading the account to the free tier. You'll get an email notification
before each retry.

Switching between monthly and annual billing takes effect at the start of
your next cycle; we don't prorate mid-cycle plan changes.
