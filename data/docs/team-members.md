# Team Members and Roles

Team (organization) accounts have three roles: Owner, Admin, and Member.
There is exactly one Owner.

Owners and Admins can invite people from Organization > Members by entering
their email address. An invitation expires after 7 days if it isn't
accepted; you can resend it from the same page.

Admins can invite and remove members and change anyone's role except the
Owner's. Only the Owner can change billing details, transfer ownership, or
delete the organization.

To transfer ownership, the Owner goes to Organization > Settings > Transfer
Ownership and picks an existing Admin. The change takes effect immediately,
and the previous Owner becomes an Admin.

Removing a member ends their access right away. Tickets they created stay
with the organization.
